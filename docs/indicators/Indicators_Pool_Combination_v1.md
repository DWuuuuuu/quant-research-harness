- **Feasibility / cost infrastructure + research moderators**, **MarketStructure (MS-I1 capacity/cost, `CanShort`, gates; MS-C3/C4 moderators; MS-R1 crowding risk-cap)**: produce **costs, capacity, vetoes, and non-directional moderators/risk-caps**: **no alpha shadow book, no standalone directional sign.** (The moderator rows *condition* another signal or *cap* risk; they never originate a long. - **This layer:** consumes the above and produces **the single net executable book**: it may **scale, veto, net, or (via a one-sided penalty) underweight a benchmark name** but **may not originate a positive directional sign** where no alpha-producing child produced one. *(A penalty underweight forces a zero-sum reallocation onto other benchmark names; that mechanical relative overweight is not an "originated long". See C4 Step 3 penalty note.)*

**Interface status: THREE distinct tracks (a research input, a mandatory legal constraint, and an operational baseline are not the same object):**
1. **Research inputs**, the promotion lifecycle: `registered` → `shadow` (computed, not sizing) → `validated` → `active` (sizes the book). Only `active` research inputs size positions; the C4 sum runs over `s ∈ ActiveAlpha_t`, each scaler/moderator/penalty enters only if `active`.
2. **`mandatory-operational`**: **genuinely legal / exchange / tradability** constraints only (`CanShort`/borrow eligibility, exchange price-limit & suspension rules, listing/eligibility bans): **always enforced, never promoted through research**, conservative missing-data fallback (unknown borrow ⇒ not-shortable. Never silently permit). **Model-dependent ADV / market-impact *capacity* is NOT here**: a days-to-liquidate or impact ceiling is an *estimated* model, so it lives in `operational-baseline` (conservative baseline) with research refinements on top. (A hard exchange halt is a legal veto; "this position is too big to exit in N days" is a model output, different tracks.)
3. **`operational-baseline`**, the models the default `κ_t`, the projection metric, and cost-gating *cannot run without*: the **beta/covariance/vol forecast** (`β(u)`, `σ̂_p`, `Σ`), the **cash leg**, the **baseline cost/impact model**, and the **realized-drawdown trigger**. Each must exist as a **conservative baseline from day one** (without `σ̂_p` the risk-budgeted default cannot be formed; without a cost model no trade can be cost-gated). A **research enhancement** to covariance or impact is a *separate research-track input* (atomic status `registered`/`shadow`/…): when it reaches `active` it **replaces** the baseline for sizing if it passed the cost-model / covariance promotion test (a validated model supersedes a conservative one), or **blends** with it under a declared shrinkage weight if the product elects blending: **declare replace-or-blend per enhancement**; the baseline is never *removed*, it remains the fallback. *(This replaces the invalid `registered→validated` cell.)*

**Feed-failure off-ramps (per track: NOT one "any state → active-stale" rule):**
- **`active` research input fails →** it goes to **`active-stale`**: its **last sleeve-target *contribution* is frozen** (`c_s·f_{s,i}` held at its last value) while **every other sleeve and the rest of the book keep updating**; the frozen contribution is **acknowledged stale exposure**: it cannot generate a *new* target but its prior weight is still in the book and still in total risk. After a bounded grace window → **`blocked-by-data`** (its contribution is dropped to the conservative default, re-entry requires re-validation). *(There is no single "portfolio holding" attributable to one signal after netting, so `active-stale` freezes the signal's **contribution**, not a slice of shares.)*
- **`registered`/`shadow`/`validated` (not yet sizing) feed fails →** straight to **`blocked-by-data`** (nothing to freeze, it wasn't sizing).
- **`operational-baseline` feed fails →** invoke its **declared fallback immediately** (last-valid within a short numerical grace, then the conservative default, e.g. shrink-to-market covariance, a wider conservative cost model); the book must not run on a broken baseline.
- **`mandatory-operational` (legal eligibility / borrow) feed fails →** **conservative mandatory block** (unknown borrow ⇒ not-shortable; missing legal-eligibility ⇒ block). Never `active-stale`. *(**Estimated capacity/impact is NOT here**, it is `operational-baseline`: a missing capacity/impact feed invokes the **operational-baseline conservative fallback** (wider impact model / tighter days-to-liquidate), not the legal block.)*

## 3. C1 (Forecast calibration (seven typed paths, causal) each routes to a *different* destination)
1. **Cross-sectional alpha → name-level tilt (`t^α`, C4 step 3):** `f_{s,i} = IC_s · σ_i · z_{s,i}` (Grinold–Kahn), `IC_s` from the sleeve's own realized rank-IC.
2. **Parent time-series alpha → `beta_cap_t` (C4 step 4), NOT the name-level `α_i` sum:** regress the parent composite on subsequent index excess return (rolling/expanding) → conditional `E_t[r−r_f]` → the beta map. **This is the one path that sets exposure, not stock tilt**. Do not add it to `Σ_i α_i`.
3. **Event alpha → name-level tilt:** calibrate on realized post-event drift by surprise bucket; tradable only after the executable timestamp.
4. **State / gate → no forecast, no budget (routed by type, NOT all into `beta_cap`):** RTI/DR/risk-appetite → the **`φ_state` severity scalar** (C4 step 4); **vol & correlation → the `σ̂_p` covariance/vol-target term** (C3, *not* `φ_state`); **realized drawdown → a C6 hard trigger, modeled DD-probability → a C6 soft cap** (neither in `φ_state`); feasibility/capacity → *vetoes/costs* (C5). None enter the expected-return vector. *(See the C4-step-4 routing table, each risk input has exactly one destination.)*
5. **Name-level one-sided risk penalty → cap/shrink (C4 step 3 penalty):** a deterioration flag (Financials **asset-quality** FIN-B10–B15/B19–B20, Fundamental F8) applies a **one-sided** shrink/cap by severity tier (not IC); worsen-only, never creates a long. *(Financials **capital** FIN-B16–B18 is a separate **risk-state/soft-cap** and **exposure**: NOT this penalty path; do not lump capital with asset-quality.)*
6. **Defensive risk allocation → two-sided tilt (`t^def`, C4 step 3):** StatRisk R-X2/R-X6 low-vol/low-beta → a **zero-sum benchmark-relative risk tilt** (up safer names AND down riskier), calibrated by the name's risk rank (not IC, and *not* a one-sided penalty, it moves weight both ways).
7. **Moderator / interaction → conditions *another* signal, NOT its own tilt (C4 step 1), typed by *which* property it moves, never a universal `f=m·f^raw`:** signals with no standalone expected return that *modulate* one. **Four mechanism-faithful sub-types (each a separate registry variant, one role per row, never "and/or"):** **7a magnitude**: scales the forecast (`f_i ← m·f_i^raw`); **7b confidence**: scales the sleeve confidence `c_s`, not the point forecast; **7c horizon**: lengthens/shortens the decay half-life `h_s` (convergence speed); **7d cap**: tightens a position/risk cap. **Assignments (split IDs, each with its own calibration/status/rejection test):** `T-X5/T-X6` info-discreteness/volume → **7a magnitude** on momentum; **`R-X7-H`** (`ARB-RISK`, *not* `T-X7`) → **7c horizon** (harder-to-arb ⇒ slower convergence) and the *separate* variant **`R-X7-M`** → **7a mispricing-magnitude** (distinct row, not "and/or" in one); **`MS-C3-H`** price-delay → **7c horizon** and the separate **`MS-C3-C`** → **7b confidence**; **`MS-C4`** news×constraint → **7b confidence** on the F5 **event** forecast. **Guards:** (1) every multiplier is **non-negative and bounded**, `0 ≤ m_min ≤ m ≤ m_max`: a moderator can never flip the sign of the forecast it conditions; (2) **multiple moderators on one signal do NOT multiply blindly** (that re-creates the correlated-compounding problem): combine via a **declared rule** (min, or a single pooled moderator per mechanism), capped; (3) inert if the conditioned signal is inactive; (4) never add to `Σ_i α_i`, never set exposure; (5) **one active application per source signal (splitting IDs does not license double use):** `R-X7-H` and `R-X7-M` draw on the *same* `R-X7`, `MS-C3-H` and `MS-C3-C` on the same `MS-C3`: **at most one of each pair may be `active` at once**, OR (if a product genuinely wants both horizon and magnitude/confidence from one source) they must pass a **joint incremental test** and thereafter **count as one mechanism against one RG / risk budget** (never two independent votes from one raw signal). This is the same mutual-exclusivity that governs `T-X7a`/`T-X7b`, extended to every role-variant pair. **`T-X7` and MS-R1 (crowding) are handled as declared variants outside a single "test which" cell**: 52-week-high is split into **`T-X7a`** (standalone x-sectional alpha, C4 step 3) and **`T-X7b`** (7a momentum magnitude moderator), mutually exclusive per RG and each separately tested; MS-R1 crowding predicts *future risk* (vol/liquidity/drawdown), so it routes to a **C6 risk cap / model-uncertainty buffer**, never a forecast multiplier. This is the "interaction path" the StatRisk/MarketStructure contracts reference. *(**ID convention:** the `-H`/`-M`/`-C`/`a`/`b` suffixes are **Combination-level role variants** of a single child source indicator: `R-X7-H`/`R-X7-M` both draw on StatRisk `R-X7`, `MS-C3-H`/`MS-C3-C` on MarketStructure `MS-C3`, `T-X7a`/`T-X7b` on TrendReversal `T-X7`; the child files own one source, this layer types the application. They are not new child IDs.)*

**Causal-OOS rule:** calibration uses only **previously-generated pseudo-OOS or live forecasts and their subsequently-realized outcomes**. Never contemporaneous or future data, and the **locked final holdout never calibrates itself.** Carry a per-sleeve confidence `c_s ∈ [0,1]` = f(OOS IC stability, sample length, regime-conditioning); low-confidence sleeves shrink toward zero, not dropped.

## 4. C4: The explicit default weight equation (the central contract)

**One master equation (benchmark → zero-sum tilts → single exposure multiplier → cash residual).** This replaces the earlier multi-step "build from positive-α then multiply by b_t" sketch, which had no benchmark, double-counted risk states, and left cash ambiguous.

Benchmark choice is **per-product, declared, not optional** (e.g. A-share: time CSI 300 for beta, but the stock-selection universe may be broader than CSI 300, state both the beta benchmark `u^bench` and the selection universe).

**Step 0, two clocks, disentangled (fixes the weekly-vs-native contradiction).** Separate the **portfolio *execution* clock** from **signal *refresh*:** (i) **execution clock = weekly**: the book re-solves every week; (ii) **signal refresh = native cadence**: a **monthly** sleeve is *not* re-ranked weekly, it is **carried and aged** (under its Step-1 decay) until its own month rolls; a **weekly** sleeve refreshes weekly; (iii) **events (F5 CAR/SUE) are queued to the next weekly execution**, and **F5 is recalibrated to the *remaining post-entry drift* from the queued execution time**, not the original event-window return (a Monday announcement traded Friday has already shed most of a 5-day PEAD; the forecast must represent what is left to capture). *(A same-day fast-path for high-conviction events is a named later extension, not the default.)* **(iv) Named cutoffs (weekly "weekly" is under-specified, pin the weekday):** normal execution is decided at a **declared weekly close** (default: **Friday market close**) and executed the **next executable session** (default: **Monday open**), per market's own calendar. **(v) Emergency override, weekly execution does NOT govern these** (they fire on the **first feasible session after the trigger**, not the next weekly slot): **realized-drawdown hard triggers (C6)**, **newly detected legal/tradability constraints (`mandatory-operational`)**, and **hard risk-limit breaches requiring the first feasible hedge (C5/C6)**. **(vi) Cross-market information cutoff:** an A-share order that is conditioned on US information uses only the **globally-complete info set available before the A-share decision cutoff** and trades the next feasible A-share session. Never same-day US data that closes after the A-share session (else look-ahead leakage; cf. CN-R8's execution-clock note).

**Step 1, moderators, then a horizon-coherent common forecast unit (fixes the decay-kernel dimension error).** First apply **path-7 moderators by their type** (magnitude → `f^raw`; confidence → `c_s`; horizon → `h_s`; cap → a C5/C6 cap), each **non-negative, bounded**, and pooled per mechanism (no blind product). Then convert every forecast to **expected active return over the coming week** with a **dimensionally-coherent horizon converter** (frozen family; numbers fitted later). Each sleeve declares its **source horizon `H_s`** (the horizon over which its raw output `F_{s,H}` is a *cumulative* expected active return) and the **volatility used in `IC·σ·z`** (same horizon). The per-week slice at signal age `a` is:
`f_{s,Δ}(a) = F_{s,H} · [ ∫_a^{min(a+Δ,H_s)} k_s(u) du ] / [ ∫_0^{H_s} k_s(u) du ]`, with `Δ` = one week, `k_s(u)=exp(−u/h_s)`.
The **denominator normalizes total mass to the original forecast** (`Σ` of weekly slices over the whole horizon = `F_{s,H}`), so this is a genuine cumulative-`H`→per-week *conversion*, not the old `f·e^{−τ/h}` that merely *aged* the number and left `H` and `σ` undefined.

**Overlapping / refreshed forecasts (each sleeve declares ONE aggregation rule: "newer > older" is insufficient and can multiply exposure across 6–12 live cohorts):**
- **(a) Latest-estimate replacement**: a new forecast *supersedes* the prior one; the old cohort's remaining mass is discarded. (Default for slow fundamental sleeves.)
- **(b) Explicit cohort portfolio**: each vintage carries a *declared capital weight*, **cohort weights summing to one** (so total exposure is never inflated by cohort count). (For genuine overlapping-holding-period sleeves, e.g. 12-1 momentum.)
- **(c) Forecast-revision model**: update the *remaining* expected-return curve of the live forecast rather than adding a second full forecast.
One rule per sleeve, declared in its contract; mixing is not allowed. Event forecasts expire at `H_s`.

**Time units:** `a`, `H_s`, `h_s`, `Δ` are all in **market-specific trading time** (trading days/weeks, not calendar), so A-share horizons skip Spring Festival / holiday closures and US horizons skip US market holidays; a sleeve may instead declare calendar time explicitly, but the unit is stated, never left ambiguous. *(Kernel family + fallback specified; `H_s`, `h_s`, `σ`, and the per-sleeve aggregation choice are research calibration.)*

**Step 2 (per-type active-status gates.** Each producer type has its own active set) **`ActiveAlpha`, `ActiveDefensive`, `ActivePenalty`, `ActiveModerator`, `ActiveState`**: plus a distinct **`mandatory-operational`** status for feasibility/legal constraints (MarketStructure `CanShort`, limits, capacity), which are **always enforced and do NOT go through research promotion**. **Conditioners are NOT a separate set, they route through `ActiveModerator`** (a conditioner is a bounded modifier of another signal, exactly the path-7 moderator mechanism): Financials `FIN-B4c`, `FIN-B16-gate`, and `FIN-AQ-SUP` all live in `ActiveModerator`. **A JOINT variant that both conditions and penalizes (e.g. `FIN-AQ-JOINT`) carries ONE atomic status** that gates both its `ActiveModerator` and `ActivePenalty` operations together, it is never independently activated in two sets. Alpha sum: `α_i = Σ_{s ∈ ActiveAlpha_t} c_s · f_{s,i}`; defensive/penalty/moderator terms below apply only over their own active sets.

**Step 3, benchmark + jointly-projected tilt (fixes the false "zero-sum by demean" and the double-σ).** `u = u^bench + t`, where the total active tilt `t` (alpha + defensive + penalty) is obtained by a **constrained projection**, not a per-name formula:
- **Risk-normalized raw active view** (diagonal MV, one σ² not one σ, the earlier `/σ_i` cancelled the `σ_i` inside `f_i=IC·σ_i·z_i`, giving un-normalized `IC·z_i`): `a_i = λ · f_i / σ_i²  (= λ·IC·z_i/σ_i, risk-normalized once)`, plus the defensive view `d_i = θ·(risk_median − risk_i)/σ_i` and one-sided penalty shrink targets.
- **Penalties are an EXPLICIT constraint, not the word "bind" (a coder must know what severity-2 means):** each penalized name `i` carries a severity `q_i ∈ [0,1]` (0 = none, 1 = full exclusion) mapping to a **post-tilt weight ceiling** `u_i ≤ (1 − q_i)·c_i`, where the **cap reference `c_i` is benchmark-relative for benchmark names but NOT benchmark weight for off-benchmark names:** `c_i = u^bench_i` when `u^bench_i > 0`, and `c_i = the name's permitted-max / raw active target` when `u^bench_i = 0`. *(Without this, an off-benchmark name has `u^bench_i=0`, so `t_i ≤ −q_i·0 = 0` would force full exclusion for **any** nonzero severity, a severity-1 and a severity-4 penalty would be identical. Binding on the raw target instead makes severity graduated for off-bench names too.)* Severity tiers → `q_i` are a declared per-source table (e.g. tier-1 `q=0.25` … tier-3 `q=1`). No penalized name may be *overweighted*.
- **Project into the long-only, benchmark-relative feasible set:** `t = argmin ‖t − (a+d)‖_Σ  s.t.  [Σ-constraint per mode below],  t_i ≥ −u^bench_i,  |t_i| ≤ max_active_i, off-bench ≤ small-cap, and the penalty ceilings `u_i ≤ (1−q_i)·c_i` above.` **Freed-weight redistribution: TWO distinct modes (they are different constructions, not interchangeable knobs):**
  - **(A) Zero-sum mode (default):** `Σ_i t_i = 0`: the penalty's removed weight is fully **redistributed across stocks**; where it lands is the **minimum-`Σ`-tracking-error** solution subject to the caps (default), or **pro-rata over unpenalized benchmark names** if a product declares that override. Gross stays at `κ_t·Σu^bench`.
  - **(B) Cash-release mode:** `Σ_i t_i ≤ 0`: the removed weight is **NOT redistributed to stocks**; the negative tilt sum `Σ_i t_i` is added to cash (`w^cash += κ_t·|Σ_i t_i|`), i.e. it reduces net stock exposure by exactly the penalized amount rather than scaling every name via `κ_t`. This is a genuinely different book from reducing `κ_t` (which scales *all* names); use it when the intent is "hold out the flagged name's weight in cash, touch nothing else."
  A product declares **one mode**; the prose "the projection determines redistribution" refers to mode (A). *(The `Σt=0` constraint is what enforces neutrality in mode (A); mode (B) relaxes it to `≤0` with the deficit booked to cash. Never leave the sum constraint unstated.)* **The unconstrained covariance-metric solution is `t* = v − Σ⁻¹𝟙 · (𝟙′v)/(𝟙′Σ⁻¹𝟙)`** (`v=a+d`): a full covariance projection, *not* a scalar subtraction; with box/off-bench/penalty constraints only the full constrained problem is exact. **The scalar-intercept root `δ` solving `Σ clip[a_i−δ, L_i, U_i]=0` is a *diagonal-only approximation*** (valid when `Σ` is treated diagonal / the metric is Euclidean), offered as a cheap warm-start, **not claimed equivalent**. *(Note `a_i` already carries `λ`: do **not** re-multiply by `λ` inside the clip; the earlier `clip[λ(a_i−δ)]` double-counted it.)*
- **Penalties act even with no active alpha/defensive tilt (frozen choice, resolves the Step-2 vs Step-3 contradiction):** an active `ActivePenalty` **may underweight a benchmark name on its own**. So: if no alpha/defensive *and* no penalty is active → `t=0` → `u=u^bench` (parent timing still holds the benchmark); **but if a penalty is active, `t≠0`**: the penalty underweights the flagged name and the **`Σt=0` constraint reallocates that freed weight across the other benchmark names**. That induced relative overweight is a **mechanical zero-sum consequence of neutrality, not an originated directional long**, which is the precise sense in which §1's "may not originate a positive sign" still holds (the penalty only ever *removes* weight from a name; where it lands is dictated by the neutrality constraint and the remaining benchmark weights, not by a new forecast).

**Step 4: ONE exposure multiplier (`beta_cap_t`, bounded).** All exposure control, once:
`κ_t = clip(min((φ_state · beta_cap_t) / β(u), base_target_vol / σ̂_p(u), scalar hard caps), 0, 1)`  *(long/flat, unlevered: `0 ≤ κ_t ≤ 1` explicit; `φ_state ∈ [scaler_min,1]` is the single validated risk-appetite state scalar defined below, renamed from `Σ_state` because `Σ` denotes the covariance; the defensive states can only *cut* exposure, never lever it)*.
**`beta_cap_t`: functional form + explicit SIGNED hysteresis state machine.** `S_t` = parent forecast Sharpe `E_t[r−r_f]/σ̂_index`; **`β_neutral` = the beta of the neutral risky composition `u`** (`u` already carries active tilts: "neutral" means the beta baseline the timing map centres on, not a tilt-free portfolio); `g` = slope; `ε_in > ε_out ≥ 0` = entry/exit half-widths. **A signed regime `r_t ∈ {−1, 0, +1}` (binary on/off was wrong, it let `sign(S_t)` flip +→− without exiting through neutral, jumping bull→bear directly):**
- enter **`+1`** when `r_{t−1}∈{0}` and `S_t ≥ ε_in`; exit `+1` (→ `0`) when `S_t ≤ ε_out`;
- enter **`−1`** when `r_{t−1}∈{0}` and `S_t ≤ −ε_in`; exit `−1` (→ `0`) when `S_t ≥ −ε_out`;
- **a direct `+1 → −1` (or `−1 → +1`) is NOT allowed**: the regime must pass through neutral `0` for **at least one decision** before entering the opposite sign (so exposure always de-risks through `β_neutral` before reversing); **initial state `r_0 = 0`** (explicit).
**Beta conditional on `r_t`:** `beta_cap_t = β_neutral + g · r_t · max(r_t·S_t − ε_out, 0)`, then `clip(·, 0, 1)`: i.e. while `r_t=+1` it is the continuous soft threshold `g·max(S_t−ε_out,0)`, while `r_t=−1` it is `g·max(−S_t−ε_out,0)` on the bearish side, and while `r_t=0` exposure is **held at `β_neutral`**. Continuous in `S_t` within each regime; the only discontinuity is the **intentional entry step at `|S_t|=ε_in`** (jump `g·(ε_in−ε_out)`), the standard cost of hysteresis: **acknowledged and to be tested**. *(`r_t` is the regime state; do not confuse it with `q_i`, the penalty severity in Step 3.)* An ordered bucket map is a fully-specified named variant. **State double-count resolved by a definitive routing table (each risk input has ONE destination):**

| Risk input | Single destination |
|---|---|
| Forecast **volatility & correlation** | the **covariance / `σ̂_p` vol-target** term (C3): **not** `φ_state` |
| **RTI / DR / independent risk-appetite** | the **`φ_state`** severity scalar |
| **Realized drawdown** | a **C6 hard trigger** |
| **Modeled drawdown probability** | a **C6 soft cap** |
| **Liquidity / funding stress** | **C5 feasibility** or a **separately-declared exposure cap** |

So `φ_state` = a min/severity-cap combination of **RTI/DR/risk-appetite only** (no vol, no correlation, no drawdown, those enter `σ̂_p` or the C6 triggers), *not* a product of correlated scalers. It is a **cap** (the `min()` can bind lower): hence `beta_cap_t`. **Default before any `active` input:** `κ_t` = the benchmark exposure that hits `base_target_vol` (risk-budgeted benchmark hold), NOT full investment.

**Step 5, positions & cash (single accounting).** `w^stock = κ_t · u`, then the **feasible-set projection**. **Which caps go where:** scalar caps (gross, beta, vol) enter the `κ_t` `min()` above; **compositional caps (name, sector, factor, liquidity, days-to-liquidate) enter the projection**: after which **β(u) and σ̂_p must be recomputed** and κ re-derived (**iterate to a declared tolerance, max-iterations cap**). **Projection objective (fixes the "min-turnover freezes the book" error, turnover must NOT be primary):** `min_w  TE(w, κ_t·u)²_Σ + γ·TC(w, w_prev)  s.t. hard constraints`: i.e. **track the target with a turnover *penalty* `γ`**, not turnover-first (a turnover-primary rule keeps the current book whenever it's merely feasible, even if the target moved a lot). The lexicographic equivalent: (1) reach a declared level of target-improvement, (2) minimize turnover among acceptable solutions. **Transaction costs enter the decision rule ONCE, here (no separate post-hoc cost gate, avoids double-penalizing turnover):** the objective is a **cost-aware target-tracking heuristic**: it trades **tracking-error reduction against cost** (`TE²` vs `γ·TC`), which is *not* the same as comparing **expected alpha** against cost. Because the target `κ_t·u` was itself built from alpha, low `γ·TC` still damps small-benefit trades, **but this does not guarantee each trade's alpha clears its cost**. Do not claim it does. *(A product that wants a literal **break-even-alpha** rule must instead optimize expected-return − risk − cost as an explicit utility, or add a break-even-alpha *constraint* and **remove** `γ·TC`; the two formulations are alternatives, never stacked.)* **"Only here" means only once in the *decision rule*, the same modeled `TC` is still deducted from *realized* strategy returns in performance accounting (C9);** costs are never absent from P&L, only never double-counted in the optimizer. **`w^cash = 1 − Σ_i w^stock_i`** (the only cash term). **Hard infeasibility is resolved by a declared relaxation hierarchy that touches ONLY soft operating bands, hard outer maxima are never relaxed:** relax, in order and each with a **declared maximum slack**, (1) the model-uncertainty buffer, (2) the **sector/factor soft operating band** (up to its hard outer max, never past it), (3) the target-improvement level. **Inviolable throughout:** legal/tradability (`mandatory-operational`), the **hard gross/net/beta max**, and the **sector/factor hard outer max**: these never receive slack. If the book is still infeasible after all *soft* slack is exhausted, it is a genuine hard breach → C5's **unresolved-breach flag + authorized executable hedge** path, logging the shortfall (never a silently-relaxed hard limit).
**Fallback (missing/unstable inputs):** if `β(u)` or `σ̂_p(u)` is missing/unstable (singular covariance), **hold the last valid exposure**; if none, **fall back to the risk-budgeted benchmark hold**, never to full investment or zero silently. **Note:** `Σ w^stock = κ_t·Σu` is a *pre-projection* identity only, compositional caps and unfilled orders change it; the executed book is whatever the projection + fill loop (§C5) produces.

Report the **1/N, inverse-vol, and ERC** benchmarks over the same object. Optimizer (max-Sharpe/max-DR) admitted only if it beats the constrained heuristic *and* ERC net-of-costs OOS; **no max-DR optimizer before diagnostic DR is validated.**

## 5. C5: Precedence, conflict resolution, and the executed-position loop
**Precedence (mapped to the C4 master equation, each risk input has EXACTLY ONE declared destination, not all in `κ_t`):** (1) **economic-driver *diagnostic/constraint***: Layer 1.5 balances growth/inflation/credit exposures as a **dashboard and exposure cap, NOT an executed multi-asset allocation** (true multi-asset All-Weather across duration/inflation/growth/commodity is **out of scope**) → (2) benchmark + zero-sum alpha tilts (C4 step 3) → (3) **single exposure multiplier `κ_t`** carrying `beta_cap_t` (parent timing sets `beta_cap`; per the C4-step-4 routing table each risk input has **one destination**: **vol & correlation → `σ̂_p`** (inside `κ_t`), **realized/modeled drawdown → C6 trigger/soft-cap** (NOT `κ_t`), **only RTI/DR/risk-appetite → the `φ_state` scalar** (inside `κ_t`): **none applied at two destinations**) → (4) feasibility loop. There is **no separate "risk-overlay scaling" step**: the earlier design double-/triple-counted the states; each now has a single declared home.

**But feasibility is NOT a one-pass afterthought, it is a loop (v1.1 fix):**
```
proposed target
  → feasibility & fill simulation (MarketStructure: CanShort, limit-lock, suspension, capacity)
  → expected executed holdings   (UNFILLED names keep their OLD position, the key point)
  → recompute normal + stressed risk on the EXECUTED book (not the intended target)
  → resize executable orders
  → final hard-limit check
```
If an untradeable holding **still breaches a hard limit**, the output is an **explicit unresolved-breach flag + an authorized executable hedge** (e.g. index futures), **not** an assumed forced de-risk that the market won't let you do.

**Conflict resolution (two sleeves, opposing signs on one name):** net by `c_s·f_{s,i}`; if the net is inside a **dead-band**, **hold the current position/target** (do NOT flatten, flattening would create the very turnover the dead-band exists to avoid) and log the disagreement into the model-uncertainty buffer (§C6).

**C5.6: Staged DR/ENB activation (removes the circularity):** at first build the combination runs on the **benchmark/risk-budget allocations only**; DR/ENB are computed in **shadow mode**, validated for incremental forecasting/risk value, and **only then** allowed to constrain or scale allocation. (Build sequence: combination = step 6, RTI test = 7, DR/ENB activation = 8, the document creates the *venue*, it does not itself validate the indicators.)

Name-level ceiling like `lottery-RB`, never a weight increase; distinct from the FIN-B16 *dividend-gate* variant (gate XOR state per product, never both) |

## 7. C3: Portfolio risk model (expanded coverage)

## 8. C8: Cash & exposure transfer function
The transfer function **is** the C4 master equation, there is no separate map (the earlier standalone equation is removed to avoid a second cash term). Risky exposure is `Σ_i w^stock_i = κ_t · Σ_i u_i` **as a pre-projection identity only**: compositional caps, the turnover-penalized projection, and unfilled orders (§C5) move the executed sum off `κ_t·Σu`; the true risky total is whatever the projection+fill loop produces. **Cash is always the single residual `w^cash = 1 − Σ_i w^stock_i` computed on the *executed* book** (not on the pre-projection target), which **earns the investable cash return** (parent US-M0 / CN-M0) and is attributed in C9. Flat periods of a long/flat strategy are cash-return periods, not zero.

## 9. C9: Attribution (must sum, with interaction residual)
Sequential/counterfactual decomposition: `total = cash + passive beta + tactical timing + Σ(each alpha sleeve) + risk-overlay effect − costs + interaction residual`. Because beta/timing/alpha/overlay interact and netting creates shared cost savings, use a **declared sequential (Shapley-style or ordered) attribution** and report the **interaction residual explicitly** rather than forcing a clean split. Timing-skill claims → the timing term only; sleeve claims → that sleeve's isolated shadow P&L. Report DR, ENB, RTI state and the driver-exposure dashboard each period.

## 10. Factor / sleeve momentum (deferred; lives here, not in stock ranking)
Once validated sleeves exist, test whether recent sleeve returns predict near-term sleeve returns, against static budgets, vol-scaling, factor-return mean-reversion, turnover/switching costs and lookback-selection risk. Deferred until the layer is live with real OOS sleeve P&L.

## 11. C7: Rebalancing
**Native cadence governs signal *refresh*; the weekly clock governs portfolio *execution* (per C4 Step 0, the two are not the same axis).** Each sleeve refreshes on its native cadence and is carried/aged between refreshes; the netted book re-solves weekly. Trade dead-bands + per-class turnover budgets. **Costs are handled inside the C4-Step-5 objective (`γ·TC`), NOT re-applied here**: MS-I1 (spread + impact + ADV participation + borrow + single-stock stamp duty / ETF-exempt) supplies the `TC` function *consumed by that objective*; C7 does not separately drop tilts on cost (that would double-count. See Step 5).

## 12. Acceptance & falsification (one primary objective)
- **Primary objective (predeclared, pick one, don't leave the choice open): net Sharpe subject to the ES/drawdown limits** (expected-utility is an alternative to be declared only if it *replaces* this, never held as a parallel option): on **common samples with identical cost treatment**. The combined book is accepted if it is **non-inferior on the primary objective and superior on tail/stability** vs (a) each individual sleeve and (b) the **ERC benchmark** (the predeclared primary benchmark; 1/N and inverse-vol are secondary). "Beat *every* sleeve on raw return" is **not** the rule, a diversified book can trail the ex-post-best sleeve on return while winning on tail/stability, and picking the ex-post-best sleeve is itself data-mining.
- Nested walk-forward, locked final holdout, block-bootstrap/HAC on overlapping targets; **the allocation method and the benchmark choice are themselves hyper-parameters under the multiple-testing discipline** (don't ship the optimizer or benchmark that only won in-sample).
- **Rejection rule:** if the constrained heuristic is not non-inferior to ERC after costs OOS, ship ERC.

## 13. Build sequence (authoritative, supersedes all per-file "build X first" notes)
**1. calendars/IDs/PIT data spine → 2. parent instrument execution + basic risk forecasts → 3. MS-I1 single-stock capacity → 4. source-faithful alpha baselines (per child) → 5. mechanism composites → 6. this combination layer (benchmark allocations) → 7. RTI promotion test → 8. DR/ENB activation → 9. licensed market-structure extensions.** The parents' "Layer 3 first" and MarketStructure's "MS-I1 first" are **local sub-sequences within their own build**, not competing global orders, this §13 is the global order.

**Open design choices (frozen architecture ≠ frozen model, these remain deliberately per-product/per-strategy, and are NOT numeric-fitting):** (a) the **benchmark `u^bench`** and selection universe chosen per product (§C4); (b) the exact **common-unit horizon-converter parameters (`H_s`, `k_s`), the turnover-cost function `TC`, and the confidence map `c_s`**: three *distinct* objects, not one (§C1/C4); (c) the **penalty severity tiers and how shrunk weight is redistributed** across the rest of the book (§3.5/C4 step 3); (d) the **moderator-interaction routing**, which path-7 signal conditions which forecast (§3.7); (e) the **conversion of scalar caps into `κ`** (which caps bind in the `min()` vs the projection, §C4 step 4/5); (f) **whether ERC/1-N diversification is applied over individual stocks or over sleeves** (§C4/§12); (g) **[flagged from `INDICATORS_REFERENCE.md` §6.4, corrected v1.13] whether factor weights should be conditioned on cross-sectional stock context (value/growth/earnings-variability), not market regime**: Sorensen–Hua–Qian (2005) report two specific composite-weight differences across high/low value and high/low growth buckets on a factor set this register already carries (`RV`≈`F1`, `OE`≈new `F2c`, `AA`≈`F3`, `EF`≈`F7`, `MO`≈`T-X1`/`F5-SUE`); their earnings-variability context was **not** clearly differentiated from the static model, and a source-faithful replication needs licensed Compustat/IBES/Barra data, not a free-data project. **This substantially overlaps item (d)** (moderator routing, `f_context = m(context)·f`) rather than requiring a wholly new mechanism, best framed as a registered stock-context × factor-interaction hypothesis, candidate for hierarchical shrinkage, not a settled architectural gap. These are open by design; fitting the *numbers* (λ, `g`, `h_s`, dead-band widths, tolerances) is the separate research task.
