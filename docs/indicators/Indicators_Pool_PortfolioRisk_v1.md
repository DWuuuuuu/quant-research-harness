## 1. RTI: Risk Tolerance Indicator (`PR-R1`; risk-state. Grades (three-dimensional, matching parents): **E:C** · **P: B/C US-global, C/D A-share-local** · **R:C**)

**TWO explicitly-different models, pick `PR-R1A` primary, `PR-R1B` comparator; NEVER blend them** (my earlier single "fixed-ordinal" rewrite was wrong, it silently *replaced* the BlackRock RTI with a different model; the fix is to keep both, correctly labelled):

**`PR-R1A`, BII-INSPIRED investable reconstruction (PRIMARY), explicitly NOT "source-faithful", and now FULLY DETERMINISTIC:** `RTI_t = SpearmanCorr_i(rank(R̄^{40d}_{i,t}), rank(σ̂_{i,t−1}))` over the **frozen 12-asset universe (§5.1)**: **exactly 12 assets; local-FX is EXCLUDED from PR-R1A** (it is not one of the 12; the EM-debt bucket is USD hard-currency only). **Frozen return weighting:** `R̄^{40d}_{i,t} = Σ_{j=0}^{39} w_j · r_{i,t−j}`, **linear-declining, most-recent-highest:** `w_j = (40−j)/820` (`Σ_{k=0}^{39}(40−k) = 820`, so `Σw_j=1`; `j=0` = the most recent daily log-return). **Frozen volatility estimator, an explicit RECURSION with a pinned seed (not the infinite sum, which a finite dataset can't evaluate and which makes results depend on how much prehistory was downloaded):** half-life 63 trading days → `λ = exp(−ln2/63) ≈ 0.98906`; **seed** `v_{s₀} = Var(r_{s₀−62: s₀})` (the trailing-63-day sample variance at the fixed burn-in date `s₀`), then the recursion `v_s = λ·v_{s−1} + (1−λ)·r²_s` for all `s > s₀`; `σ̂_{i,t−1} = √(v_{t−1}·252)`. **Burn-in convention frozen:** each asset's series starts at a declared `s₀` = its first date with ≥63 prior returns; the RTI series itself begins once **all 12 assets** have passed burn-in. *(Resolves "63d = half-life/span/window": it is the **half-life**; and "sample-var init vs infinite sum": the recursion with the 63d seed is the single implementation.)*
**All-12-required rule (no silent shrinkage):** `RTI_t` is computed **only when all 12 assets are available** that date; if **any** of the 12 is missing/NA, `RTI_t = NA` (it must never silently become an 11-asset statistic). **Documented proxy differences from BlackRock** (why this is a reconstruction, not a replication): gold vs BlackRock's **listed private equity**; USD hard-currency EM-debt (EMBI-style) vs any local-FX; **US small-cap** here vs BlackRock's **developed-market small-cap**; DM-currency index mix differs; and BlackRock discloses only "a weighted MA over 40 days" and "annualized volatility": **not** the exact weights or estimator, so the linear-`w_j` and 63d-half-life EWMA above are **our declared choices, not BlackRock's**. **A genuinely source-faithful RTI (exact indices/currencies/estimator/weights) is `blocked-by-data`** (proprietary). The vol rank axis means a defensive asset in a volatile month *can* rank "risky", inherent to the RTI, hence extremes-only, always paired with an absolute state.

**`PR-R1B`, fixed economic-risk-ordinal leadership (COMPARATOR, a DISTINCT model, NOT the RTI):** `RTI^{ord}_t = SpearmanCorr_i(rank(R̄^{40d}_{i,t}), Ρ_i)` with `Ρ_i` a **fully-frozen ordinal over ALL 12 assets** (defensive→cyclical, rank 1 = most defensive): **1 gold · 2 government duration · 3 inflation-linked bonds · 4 IG credit · 5 listed real estate · 6 infrastructure · 7 DM equity · 8 EM-debt (hard-ccy) · 9 US small-cap · 10 HY credit · 11 commodities · 12 EM equity**; **ties: none permitted, the 12 ranks are distinct** (if two assets are judged equal, the lower-numbered slot is declared and frozen). It reads cleaner but is **regime-naive**: a fixed ordinal ignores that commodities/duration/gold/local-FX roles are regime-dependent (commodities cyclical in demand booms but a supply-shock hedge; duration defensive except when inflation makes bonds risky, 2022). Comparator only, never "the RTI."

**Common construction (both models):**
- **ONE frozen primary universe = the 12-asset §5.1 concentration universe** (DM equity, US small-cap, EM equity, government duration, ILB, IG credit, HY credit, EM debt, commodities, gold, listed RE, infrastructure), one liquid total-return instrument per asset, cap representation per bucket. *(This is the RTI universe; the DR/ENB diagnostic in §2 uses the same breadth but is a different computation. Do not conflate the two objects.)*
- **Currency (per-bucket, declared):** returns in one declared base currency; **developed foreign buckets FX-hedged**; the **EM-debt bucket is USD hard-currency, hedged to base**. **Local-FX is NOT part of the 12-asset PR-R1A universe** (it would create a 13th bucket): if a product wants a currency-risk read it is a *separate* diagnostic, not an RTI input.
- **Positive** = risky assets led → risk-on; **negative** = flight-to-quality; **near-zero** = no monotonic ordering. Associated with Fergis et al. *Defensive Factor Timing* (an **extreme defensive overlay**, not a continuous forecaster).

**What it is NOT:** equity momentum, VIX, average asset return, survey sentiment, expected return, or an absolute market-direction call. Two edge cases force pairing with an absolute state variable:
1. RTI **positive while everything falls** (risky falls less than safe);
2. RTI **negative while everything rises** (safe rises more).
→ **RTI never independently sets long-vs-cash.** Always pair with risky-sleeve trend / drawdown / realized-implied vol / credit-funding stress.

**Use only extremes, via rolling percentile, not the raw continuous level** (with the 12-asset universe the Spearman sampling distribution is wide; +0.3 vs −0.3 may be statistically indistinguishable; middle range is noise).

**⚠ The binding gate (v1, my emphasis): incremental value over signals we ALREADY own.** RTI is essentially a rank-summary of risk-on/off state already carried by **US-R8** (bond-equity corr), **US-X3** (defensive-vs-cyclical leadership), **US-M2 / US-X1** (credit), **US-R1–R4** (vol states), **US-X4** (gold/haven): A-share analogues **CN-R7 / CN-X4 / CN-X2 / CN-R1–R2**. Honest prior = *mostly redundant*. → **Enter RTI first as a dashboard *consolidation* of those signals (one interpretable risk-state number); promote it to an independent input only if it beats the signals it summarizes out-of-sample.** Its likely value is interpretability/consolidation, not new information.

**Failure modes to design around:** small cross-section (rank flips on 1–2 markets); universe dependence (redundant equity proxies dominate the ranking → **cap representation per asset bucket**); risk-ranking endogeneity (an asset is labelled "high-risk" only *after* it crashes and realized vol rises); low-vol anomaly (higher vol ≠ higher expected return); currency basis (hedged vs unhedged); non-synchronous closes; proxy inception dates; **overlap with existing trend/credit/haven/vol indicators**.

**Benchmark, don't merge:** a broad PCA risk-appetite index (Bauer–Bernanke–Milstein) is a worthwhile comparator but a *different model*. Never averaged mechanically with RTI. (ECB: the 1st PC of 14 risk-appetite indicators explains only ~38% of their variation: "risk appetite" is not one clean observable.)

## 2. DR & diversification diagnostics (dormant until §4 trigger)

### PR-D1: Diversification Ratio (identity **E:A** · timing **E:C**)
`DR(w) = (Σ_i w_i σ_i) / sqrt(wᵀΣw)`: weighted-average standalone vol / actual portfolio vol (Choueifaty–Coignard). DR=1 → no benefit; higher → more vol reduction. **Diagnostic, not alpha:** high DR ≠ high forward return; falling DR ≠ automatically bearish (correlations can rise in a bull; a low-vol asset can dominate risk; vol moves faster than correlation; noise). **Forecast portfolio risk / diversification failure. Never score as directional alpha.**

**⚠ Degenerate for the current parents (v1, sharpening): for a true index+cash portfolio, DR ≡ 1 *exactly*** (cash σ≈0 contributes nothing to numerator or portfolio vol → `w·σ/(w·σ)=1` for any risky weight). So DR conveys **zero** information about holding 30/60/100% of one index. Also: multiple timing models that all create SPY/CSI300 exposure are **one market-beta source**: treating them as separate "assets" fabricates diversification.

**⚠ Admissible weight domain (v1): the DR identity and the PR-D2 concentration/correlation decomposition assume NONNEGATIVE risk allocations.** With negative sleeve weights (hedged/short books admitted at the §4 activation stage), `Σ w_iσ_i`, the risk-share Herfindahl `CR` and the average-correlation form lose their stated meaning. Rule: **DR/PR-D2 are computed only over nonnegative sleeve allocations; hedged/short books use a separately-defined gross-risk or factor-risk-contribution version.**

### PR-D2: DR concentration/correlation decomposition (**E:A**)
`DR = [CR + (1−CR)ρ̄]^(−1/2)`, where `a_i = w_iσ_i`, `CR = Σ_i (a_i/Σa)²` (Herfindahl of risk shares), `ρ̄` = vol-weighted average correlation. **DR can fall for two economically different reasons → different responses:** (1) **concentration up** (CR↑, one sleeve dominates) → *rebalance*; (2) **correlations up** (ρ̄↑, sleeves converge) → *cut gross / question the hedge architecture*. A single DR number is insufficient; always report the decomposition.

### PR-D3: Scenario-stress DR (**E:C**)
Ordinary DR is an **average-state** covariance measure; correlations turn asymmetric in falling markets (Longin–Solnik; Ang–Chen), so a normal Σ **overstates crisis protection**. **Preference (v1, my refinement): use *scenario-imposed* stressed correlations rather than an *empirically-estimated* downside/exceedance covariance**: the latter is data-starved (a covariance on only down-market days is noisy). **PSD-safe transformation (freeze it, don't leave it as a tuning DOF): convex blend toward a pre-specified PSD target correlation matrix, `R_stress = (1−λ)R + λ·R_target`, with pre-registered `λ∈[0,1]`.** Two admissible targets: (a) **uniform crisis** `R_target = 11ᵀ` (all correlations → 1; already carries a negative stock–bond correlation *through zero toward +1*, so no separate "sign-flip" clause is needed or allowed, a pair-specific override would break PSD); (b) **heterogeneous crisis**: a *separately specified PSD* `R_target` (e.g. stock–bond set positive, intra-equity near 1), which is the clean way to impose non-uniform stress while preserving PSD. **Marginal-volatility stress is a separate pre-registered choice** (stress vols or hold them), not implied by the correlation blend. A parametric asymmetric model is an acceptable alternative; naive empirical-downside DR is not the default.

### PR-D4 (Effective Number of Bets / risk-contribution concentration (**E:B) CO-primary with DR (not "preferred"), answering a different question**)
`ENB = exp(−Σ_k p_k ln p_k)` over the risk contributions of **uncorrelated minimum-torsion risk sources** (Meucci); `p_k = w²_{MT,k}σ²_{MT,k}/σ²_p` (guarantees `p_k≥0, Σp_k=1`). ENB exposes the "many models → one market beta" problem more naturally than the weighted-vol-ratio DR: **but it is NOT strictly "preferred": it is sensitive to covariance estimation, factor basis, orthogonalization choice and unstable eigenvectors.** So **co-primary with DR, answering different questions** (DR = vol-reduction from the raw weights; ENB = number of independent bets), and neither replaces the economic-driver dashboard. **ENB reported as THREE numbers, not one, so its level can't be gamed by how many synthetic residual dimensions you invent (the key freeze):** because "residual = one synthetic bet" vs "every security residual = its own bet" changes ENB by an order of magnitude, ENB is **not** reported as a single scalar over an ad-hoc residual basis. Instead: **(a) systematic-factor ENB**: the min-torsion ENB computed **over the declared risk-model FACTORS only** (`r` = risk-model factor returns, `w = β = Bᵀw_stock`); **(b) residual-risk share of total variance** `= w_stock′ D w_stock / (w_stock′Σ_total w_stock)` (`D` = idiosyncratic variances); **(c) residual concentration HHI, exact formula:** for the diagonal idiosyncratic matrix `D`, per-name residual-variance share `q_i = w²_{stock,i}·D_{ii} / (w_stock′ D w_stock)` and `HHI_ε = Σ_i q_i²`; **zero-residual-risk convention:** if `w_stock′ D w_stock = 0`, `HHI_ε` is **NA** (there is no residual risk to concentrate). These three outputs are **complementary** (not statistically "orthogonal"): systematic diversification, residual share, residual concentration, none dependent on a fabricated residual dimensionality. **Min-torsion (for (a), factor space only):** bets `t = T·r` (`cov(t)=diag`, `T` closest to `r` in correlation space per the **Meucci–Santangelo–Deguest MLT iteration on `C=σ⁻¹Σσ⁻¹`, rescaled by `σ`**: not Cholesky/PCA); exposures `w_MT = T⁻ᵀ·β`; `p_k = w²_{MT,k}σ²_{MT,k}/(β′Σβ)`. **Pin the implementation exactly:** the reference release is the **Meucci MLT MATLAB-FEX release v1.3.0.0** (v1.2 carried the transpose correction) and the matrix orientation is declared; **the portfolio-return identity `β′r = w_MT′t` and the transform orientation MUST BE tested in the paper-test harness** (they cannot be asserted before code/fixtures exist, this is a required test, not a claimed result); `Σ` = the C3 **Ledoit–Wolf** estimator; **singular/near-singular `Σ` → shrink to the LW target before inverting `Tᵀ`** (one declared rule); tolerance/max-iter/conditioning floor declared at calibration. **Open dependency:** the systematic-ENB (a) is computed over the C3 **factor basis, which is NOT yet frozen** (Combination C3 says "Ledoit–Wolf / factor" without declaring the factor set), so the **production ENB *number* is pending the C3 factor-covariance freeze**; a synthetic factor basis suffices for the orientation/identity *test*, but the live value is not yet fully determined.

### PR-T1: Portfolio turbulence & absorption ratio (**E:C, deferred robustness**)
Turbulence = Mahalanobis distance of the current joint return vector vs its history (Kritzman–Li, flags unusual *convergence/decoupling*, not just large single moves; the third monitor BlackRock shows beside RTI/DR). Absorption ratio = fraction of total variance in the top principal components (Kritzman–Li–Page–Rigobon, market-wide concentration comparator to portfolio-specific DR). **Deferred** monitors; admit only after RTI + DR/ENB earn their place.

### Not duplicated
Parent correlation indicators stay in the parents (**US-R5** implied/avg correlation; **CN-R3** dispersion & avg pairwise correlation). Those describe the *market*; DR/ENB describe the consequence for the *portfolio's actual weights*, different objects, keep both.

## 3. RTI × DR four-state policy (research hypothesis, thresholds in nested training, tested on holdout)

| State | Reading | Candidate response |
|---|---|---|
| High RTI, high DR | risk rewarded; diversification working | normal target risk |
| **High RTI, low DR** | risk-on but crowded → portfolio becoming one trade | limit gross/concentration; **do NOT auto-reverse alpha**. **← portfolio-level echo of the stock-level `CrowdingFragilityScore` (MarketStructure MS-R1): cross-reference them; same fragility at two scales** |
| Low RTI, high DR | flight-to-quality but hedges still work | moderate tactical reduction / defensive rotation |
| Low RTI, low DR | risk-off + diversification failure | strongest defensive state |

BlackRock's illustrative ~20% risk cut at extremes is a **benchmark scenario, not a number to copy.** The four-state map is coherent but unproven; it is not permitted to originate direction where no independent alpha exists.

**⚠ The table is NOT a standalone exposure rule (fixes an internal contradiction): RTI is relative, DR is a diagnostic (and dormant for index+cash), so neither the table nor the two together can set exposure.** The actual rule is conditioned on an **absolute state**:
this state is one input to the **single validated risk-appetite scalar** that feeds Combination's exposure multiplier: **it does NOT set exposure on its own.** In Combination vocabulary the scalar is **`φ_state ∈ [scaler_min,1]`**, and **per Combination's C4-step-4 routing table it is built from RTI / DR / risk-appetite ONLY**: `φ_state(RTI, DR/ENB, independent risk-appetite) → κ_t` (see Combination C4 step 4). **Volatility, correlation, liquidity and funding are NOT `φ_state` inputs:** forecast vol & correlation route to the covariance / `σ̂_p` vol-target term, realized/modeled drawdown to the C6 hard-trigger / soft-cap, and liquidity/funding stress to C5 feasibility or a separately-declared exposure cap, each risk input has exactly one destination, so nothing is double-counted. *(Earlier drafts called the scalar `RiskTarget` and folded vol/liquidity/funding into it; the master equation now routes those elsewhere and there is no separate risk-target object.)*
Worked examples: *low RTI + low DR + **negative** absolute risky-sleeve trend* → strongest defensive state; *low RTI + low DR but **positive** absolute trend* → concentration **warning**, not an automatic exit. The four-state cells are read only *within* the absolute-state condition.

DR/ENB are **information-free for the current index+cash parents** (PR-D1: DR≡1). They become real only at the **multi-sleeve strategy-combination layer, now specified in `Indicators_Pool_Combination_v1.md`, where staged activation lives in §C5.6 (shadow → validate → constrain).** The natural trigger is the first of:
- the **alpha-producing** cross-sectional children (Fundamental value/quality/event rows, TrendReversal, Financials value/profitability) combined into one book with **separately-measured sleeve P&L**. Note **StatRisk is NOT an alpha child** (its lottery rows are an avoid-screen and R-X2/R-X6 are a defensive risk-tilt, both non-alpha per Combination §1/C4), and **MarketStructure is NOT an alpha child** either (costs/gates plus non-directional research moderators/risk-caps: MS-C3/C4 moderators and MS-R1 crowding cap, never a directional sign);

Until then, DR/ENB stay **dashboard diagnostics only**. **RTI/DR are `registered` (specified, not yet computed): NOT `shadow`, which under the suite's own status ontology means *already emitting causal shadow outputs*.** Once actually computed they become `shadow`, then must pass the incremental-value test and promote to `validated → active` before they may scale executable exposure (no input sizes the book until `active`).

## 6. Acceptance tests (evaluate against future RISK, not return)

**RTI:** next-21d realized vol; 63d drawdown probability; max future drawdown; expected shortfall; defensive-hedge performance; **false-positive de-risking cost.**
**DR/ENB:** does forecast DR predict *realized* diversification?; future portfolio vol *beyond* the current vol forecast; future ES; hedge failure; **incremental information beyond average correlation (US-R5/CN-R3) and risk concentration.**
**Mandatory ablations:** (1) existing Layer 3 only; (2) +RTI; (3) +DR; (4) +RTI+DR; (5) RTI/DR vs correlation-only alternatives.
Overlapping windows + rare extremes → **block-bootstrap/HAC errors, event-level reporting, locked final holdout.**

> **Rejection rule:** if RTI or DR does not reduce OOS drawdown/tail risk after costs (or its benefit is fully explained by existing vol/trend/correlation measures) it stays a **dashboard descriptor**, not an exposure control.
