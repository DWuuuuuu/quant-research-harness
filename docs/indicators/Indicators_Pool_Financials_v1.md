# Financial-Sector Factor Contracts: A-Share (US = research agenda), v1.13 (Child Contract)

**Scope (current):** **Bank / Insurer / Broker only: REITs are OUT OF SCOPE** (§4, deferred; rationale retained there). **A-share native**; the US section (§10) is a **research agenda, not contracted rows**.
**Parent files:** `Indicators_Pool_US_v2.md`, `Indicators_Pool_AShare_v2.md`. Sibling of `Indicators_Pool_Fundamental_v1.md` (which excludes financials from its *industrial* F1–F8, this document owns them). Execution/capacity via MS-I1; allocation via `Indicators_Pool_Combination_v1.md`.
**Why a separate child:** the industrial F1–F8 formulas are **invalid for financials**: net-debt/EBITDA, EV/CFO, current ratio, accruals, CFO/NI have no clean meaning when debt/leverage *is* the business (banks/insurers) or cash flow is regulatory. Each sub-sector gets sector-native primitives.
**Bank priority is justified by** economic/credit-cycle importance + distinct accounting + a **material index weight** (the [official CSI 300 factsheet](https://oss-ch.csindex.com.cn/static/html/csindex/public/uploads/indices/detail/files/en/000300factsheeten.pdf) gives **Financials = 17.1% of CSI 300 as of 2026-06-30**; banks are the largest financial sub-sector, a precise bank % needs a dated constituent-weight sum, so no bank-only number is attached; **not** the earlier erroneous "30–40%") + the inadequacy of ordinary industrial valuation: **not** a single headline statistic.

**Maturity (honest, this is NOT a frozen per-indicator contract):**
- **A-share banks (§2):** an **advanced factor-family draft**: per-indicator IDs, formulas, signs, output types, PIT lags, missingness rules and acceptance tests are specified, but a few estimators (sustainable ROE, normalized credit cost, COE) are still **named-but-unfitted**, so the family is not frozen.
- **Insurers (§3) / brokers (§5):** **`registered` diagnostic outlines**: ID'd, but thin disclosure / tiny cross-section / applicability splits keep them diagnostics, not sized signals.
- **US (§10):** **research agenda only**: IDs reserved, no contracted rows.
- **Legal/tradability gates:** **owned centrally by MarketStructure** (`mandatory-operational`); this child **contributes no live legal veto**: it only *reserves a schema* (§2.6, no active instance) for a future financial-sector-specific prohibition, and treats capital weakness / probable intervention as **research penalties, not legal vetoes.**

**Combination interface status (corrected to Combination's own definitions):** research rows are **`registered`** (specified, **not yet computed**: no Financials strategy is implemented), NOT `shadow` (which means *already producing causal shadow outputs*). Rows become `shadow` only once implemented and emitting shadow diagnostics; presently-infeasible rows (e.g. undisclosed ALM gap) are **`blocked-by-data`**.

## 1b. Redundancy-group (RG) dictionary
One composite per **mechanism**; correlation/ablation tracked *within* group; never two same-RG rows as diversification.
- `FIN-VALUE-BANK`: bank capital-quality-adjusted valuation (**risk-adjusted valuation hypothesis, NOT automatic mispricing**: a cheap residual may be fair compensation for unmeasured asset quality / default risk).
- `FIN-EARNDYN-BANK`: bank earnings power & **dynamics** (ROA/NIM/efficiency/fee-share **and excess-ROE + Δexcess-ROE**, moved here from value, they are profitability, not valuation; `E/P ≈ B/P×ROE` is used as a *decomposition*, not a third vote).
- `FIN-ASSETQ-BANK`: bank asset-quality / credit-loss complex (FIN-B10–B15): **one-sided screen**, not alpha; owns the `q_AQ` budget.
- **Three *distinct* non-asset-quality bank screens** (different mechanisms/horizons/targets: NOT one composite): `FIN-GROWTH-BANK` (excess-loan-growth / underwriting risk, FIN-B19 → forward NPL), `FIN-GOV-BANK` (governance / connected transactions, FIN-B20a → tail/fraud risk), `FIN-CAPACT-BANK` (dilution / capital-action event, FIN-B20b/c → event overhang). Each is its own RG/budget; a **higher-level total name-level cap** bounds their *combined* penalty so they can't stack without limit.
- `FIN-CAPITAL-BANK`: bank capital adequacy & funding.
- `FIN-SIZE-BANK`: **bank size / systemic-importance exposure** (bank-specific size effects are not captured by ordinary equity size factors, Gandhi–Lustig; a control/exposure, not alpha).
- `FIN-VALUE-INS` · `FIN-EARNDYN-INS` · `FIN-SOLV-INS` · `FIN-ALM-INS` · `FIN-ACCTQ-INS`: insurer value / new-business & underwriting / solvency / rate-duration / accounting-quality.
- `FIN-VALUE-BROKER` · `FIN-CAPITAL-BROKER` · `FIN-CYCLE-BROKER`: broker normalized value / net-capital / market-cycle exposure.
- **Cross-child overlaps (redundancy-test, never additive):** `FIN-VALUE-*` ≠ Fundamental `VALUE` (F1); bank/insurer dividend yield ↔ parent **CN-V3**; broker cyclicality ↔ parent **CN-B2**; insurer value ↔ Layer-1.5 long-rate/equity regime.

### 2.1 Value, risk-adjusted valuation (RG `FIN-VALUE-BANK`; output **alpha**)
Raw P/B is **incomplete, not meaningless**. The residual-income intuition `P/B ≈ (ROE−g)/(COE−g)` holds only in steady state; `COE`/sustainable-ROE/`g` are noisy, so an *estimated* "justified P/B" is **not** clean fair value. **Frame FIN-B1 as a risk-adjusted valuation *hypothesis*, not automatic mispricing**: a cheap residual may be compensation for unmeasured asset quality/default risk (Q-theory of banks), which is exactly why it is gated by the asset-quality screen.

The same name-state never triggers both; the choice is fixed per product, not selected per name (which would double-count FIN-B16).
**Asset-quality interaction: ONE mechanism, EXACTLY ONE predeclared mode, everything reconciled in ONE unit (post-tilt weight CEILING, not a mix of severity and weight):** the previous `max(q_AQ, q_sup)` was dimensionally invalid (`q_AQ` is a unitless severity, `q_sup` a weight, and `w_B1` could be negative). Fixed by expressing **both** expressions as a **post-tilt weight ceiling** on the name and taking the **tighter (lower) ceiling.** Let `c_i` be the name's cap reference (its benchmark weight, or its permitted-max for off-benchmark names, same `c_i` Combination's penalty uses; if `c_i = 0` the name cannot be over-weighted anyway, so the ceiling is trivially 0):
- **Mode A `FIN-AQ-PEN` (penalty only, default):** ceiling `= (1 − q_AQ)·c_i` (`q_AQ ∈ [0,1]` the unitless severity from §2.3); FIN-B1 flows unmodified into the pre-cap target.
- **Mode B `FIN-AQ-SUP` (suppression only):** FIN-B1's contribution to the pre-cap target is scaled by `(1 − s_AQ)`, `s_AQ ∈ [0,1]`; **no explicit ceiling** (the effect is already in the target).
- **Mode C `FIN-AQ-JOINT` (joint, ONE ceiling via a TWO-PASS COUNTERFACTUAL, non-circular):** the earlier `u_i^{sup}` was self-referential (it was "the weight the projection produces", used as a ceiling *inside* that same projection) and let both effects act. Fixed: **before** the live solve, run **two throwaway counterfactual books**: a *penalty-only* book (yields weight `u_i^{PEN}` under ceiling `(1−q_AQ)·c_i`) and a *suppression-only* book (yields `u_i^{SUP}` with FIN-B1 scaled by `1−s_AQ`, no ceiling). Mode C then binds the live solve with **ONE fixed constant ceiling** `c_i^{JOINT} = min(u_i^{PEN}, u_i^{SUP})`: a **number computed before** the live projection, not a function of it, and **FIN-B1 is NOT re-suppressed in the live objective** (the suppression already entered `u_i^{SUP}`; re-applying it would double-count). One deduction, one ceiling, no circularity. Mode C is used **only if** the joint incremental test clears; else fall back to Mode A.
Exactly one of `FIN-AQ-PEN` / `FIN-AQ-SUP` / `FIN-AQ-JOINT` is eligible per product, enforced as mutually-exclusive role-variant statuses; **`FIN-AQ-JOINT` carries ONE atomic Combination status** (it is never independently activated as both a conditioner and a penalty).

### 2.3 Asset quality, one-sided screen (RG `FIN-ASSETQ-BANK`; output **screen/penalty**, target = risk not return)
Deterioration flags shrink/cap weight by severity tier (Combination C1 penalty §3.5); never create a long. **ONE frozen primary target, a single continuous variable:** `y_{i,t} = ΔNPL_{i, t→t+8q}` (the 2-year change in NPL ratio), with **current NPL, peer group and credit regime entered as controls** (so the composite is judged on *incremental* deterioration, not the persistent level it already contains). **THE metric: ONE frozen expression (`ΔIC_OOS`), a FORMATION-DATE cross-sectional IC (NOT a pooled bank-time correlation, which would let credit-regime/time variation dominate a cross-sectional bank screen), used for selection, calibration AND promotion:**
`ΔIC_OOS = (1/T)·Σ_t [ ρ_{S,i}(ŷ⁽¹⁾_{i,t}, y_{i,t}) − ρ_{S,i}(ŷ⁽⁰⁾_{i,t}, y_{i,t}) ]`: the Spearman rank-IC is computed **across banks *within* each formation date `t`**, then **equal-date averaged** over the `T` formation dates (equal-date, not observation-weighted, so no single crowded date dominates). `ŷ⁽⁰⁾` = out-of-fold prediction of a **controls-only** model (current NPL + peer + regime); `ŷ⁽¹⁾` = **controls + composite**; **same fixed estimator, observations and folds for both.** **The estimator is named and frozen: a cross-sectional OLS on within-peer-group-demeaned z-scores** (Model-1 adds the composite z as one regressor); the *same* OLS spec for both models. **Declared handling:** minimum **8 eligible banks per date** (dates below → dropped from the average, not zero-filled); **peer-neutralization = within-peer-group demean before the IC** (matches §9); **ties → average ranks**; **undefined date-IC (constant `ŷ` or `y`, or <8 banks) → that date is excluded**, not scored 0. Raw pooled `ρ_S(composite, y)` is **NOT** the metric (the composite contains current NPL, which self-predicts `y`, and pooling smuggles in time variation). **This is "incremental OOS predictive value," NOT "causal"**: out-of-time prediction with controls establishes incremental predictive value, not causality; the word "causal" is dropped. **Δ-deviance and the residual-target form are robustness checks only, never the selection metric.** **A discrete deterioration event and the forward NPL *level* are SECONDARY diagnostics only**: they are different models/metrics and cannot substitute for the frozen `ΔNPL` primary. Forward credit-cost and downside-return remain secondary. **ΔNPL is a NOISY target, it moves through charge-offs, disposals/ABS, cures, reclassification, and loan-book (denominator) growth, not just true deterioration.** Before promotion, the composite must be **cross-validated for consistency with the other credit signals**: NPL **net formation** (FIN-B14b = `G − C = ΔNPL + S + W`: the flow that *adds write-offs and disposals back* and nets cures, with post-write-off recoveries excluded to net charge-offs, per the corrected identity), **special-mention migration** (FIN-B11, the leading indicator), and **credit cost** (FIN-B13): a "deterioration" prediction contradicted by flat net-formation and falling special-mention is likely a denominator/disposal artifact. **Treat these three as a REQUIRED consistency panel at promotion (a robustness gate), NOT an automatic per-name veto**: when a panel field is missing or uses a non-comparable vendor definition, flag it and down-weight confidence, do not hard-reject the name.

**Composite: FROZEN STRUCTURE, parameters fitted per the protocol below (rule shape frozen; `‹fit›` numbers set once on the training window, then locked):**
- **PRIMARY score = FIXED CORE ONLY (comparable across all banks): core = {FIN-B10 NPL level, FIN-B10Δ NPL change, raw reported provision-coverage *level*}**: the only always-disclosed A-share fields. The primary score uses **exactly these three, for every bank**, so a bank's score never changes merely because another field becomes available. *(NOT the FIN-B12 buffer basis (a), which needs the often-ND requirement; NOT FIN-B11 special-mention.)*
- **Core-universality caveat (insufficient history = NA, not ND):** FIN-B10Δ needs 5 quarters but the universe only seasons IPOs 6 months, so a *newly-listed* bank can be eligible yet lack a core field. This is **`NA` (genuinely absent), NOT non-disclosure**: it must not trigger the disclosure penalty. Such a bank is either **held out of asset-quality eligibility until it has 5 quarters**, or scored on a **separately-labelled reduced-core baseline** {FIN-B10 level, coverage level} (declared per product). Never silently missing a mandatory core field.
- **EXTENDED score (separate, labelled robustness. Never the primary): all-available** = core + whatever add-ons (B11, B12(a/b), B13, B14, B15) are disclosed. Reported alongside the primary to check whether extra disclosure adds signal, but positions size on the **primary core-only** score.
- **ABSOLUTE deterioration ACTIVATES the tier; relative rank only SCALES severity** (fixes the system-wide-deterioration blind spot, requiring *both* absolute AND worst-decile left objectively-weak banks uncapped when the *whole* group deteriorated and none was relatively "worst"): a component fires **whenever it is past its declared absolute threshold `‹fit›`** (NPL YoY rise ≥ `‹fit›bps`; raw coverage below `‹fit›%`): **regardless of peer rank.** Peer rank then **multiplies the severity** (worst-ranked → higher tier), but is **never a gate**. A healthy bank below all absolute thresholds fires nothing; a broadly-weak group has *all* its objectively-weak banks capped, correctly. *(Small groups: rank via the §9 pooled-residual percentile with shrinkage; ties → less-severe.)*
- **`q_AQ` has NO `q=1` route at all, asset quality is an economic signal, and an economic signal is never a legal exclusion.** Tiers: tier-1 `q=0.25`, tier-2 `q=0.5`; the aggregate is **strictly capped `q_cap < 1`**, so even maximal economic distress (coverage<100% + rising NPL + worst rank) produces a **high capped penalty, never full exclusion**: `severity = min(q_cap‹fit›, Σ_core min(w_i·tier_i, κ_i))`, each per-component cap bounded `κ_i ≤ ρ·q_cap`, predeclared `ρ < 1`.
- **Full exclusion is a LEGAL fact routed through the RIGHT interface: NOT through `q_AQ`:** an **enumerated legal holding/trading prohibition** (a regulator order that actually bars holding the share) is a **MarketStructure feasibility veto** that sets eligibility / maximum weight to zero (the reserved `FIN-BGATE` schema, MarketStructure-owned), entirely outside `q_AQ`. **A supervisory downgrade or capital intervention that does NOT prohibit holding is an economic risk signal** and stays within the capped `q_AQ` above (it does not make the listed share illegal to hold). The old "legal `q=1` bypasses `q_cap`" wording is removed: `q_AQ` never reaches 1, and legal exclusion lives in MarketStructure, not the asset-quality penalty.
- **Calibration contract (the `‹fit›` set, thresholds, core weights `w_i`, per-component caps `κ_i`, `q_cap`, `ρ`, `λ`):** **(objective)** maximize **`ΔIC_OOS`** (§2.3, the *incremental* out-of-fold Spearman-IC of controls+composite over controls-only, NOT raw rank-IC against `y`) **minus a turnover/opportunity-cost term** `λ·turnover`; **(selection)** nested **walk-forward** (inner fold selects `‹fit›`, outer fold scores): **the locked holdout never selects parameters**; **(regularization)** prefer the **simplest** model within one SE of the best (tie-break to fewer active components / rounder thresholds); **(min-effect)** must clear a predeclared **`ΔIC_OOS`** hurdle over the controls-only (core-NPL) baseline or it ships the baseline. **The numeric bounds themselves (the bps threshold ladder, the `κ_i`/`ρ` bounds, `λ`, the min-incremental-IC hurdle, `q_cap` grid) are declared at calibration and are the *parameter-pending* content**: the protocol shape is frozen, the numbers are not yet set, and this is stated honestly (not "fully declared"). Until it runs, the composite is **structure-frozen, parameters-pending**: not a live number.
- **Inference contract for the screen (OWN block length, the §1 alpha inference is ~12-month; this target is 24-month):** the `ΔNPL_{t→t+8q}` target overlaps **8 quarters / 24 months**, so its effective temporal sample is *smaller* than the alpha rows'. Use an **8-quarter block length** in the block-bootstrap, retain **bank × formation-time two-way clustering**, and, because the A-share listed-bank cross-section has **few clusters** (~40 banks): add a **wild-cluster bootstrap** (or 2-D block bootstrap) robustness test; report the effective sample size and qualify on thin regime coverage.
- **Disclosure handled separately** (§1): non-disclosure → disclosure/model-uncertainty soft cap, never into this severity.
- **ONE mechanism with FIN-B1**: §2.1 modes `FIN-AQ-PEN/SUP/JOINT` (single weight ceiling), never two deductions.
